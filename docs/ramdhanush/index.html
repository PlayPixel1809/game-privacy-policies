Game privacy policies

Ram Dhanush Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2 November 2026

This policy explains how the developer of Ram Dhanush, a Ramayana-inspired Android archery game, handles information when you use the game. It covers the game's local data, online leaderboards, advertising services and support requests.

Privacy contact: playpixelinteractive1809@gmail.com.

1. Game data stored on your device

Ram Dhanush stores game progress, best scores, coins, upgrades, avatar choices, your chosen profile name and sound preferences on your device. Your profile name is entered voluntarily; you do not need to use your real name. The current game does not ask you to supply an email address, phone number, password or payment-card information to play.

2. Online leaderboard information

The game uses Microsoft PlayFab for online leaderboards. When online features are used, PlayFab can process an installation-specific identifier, a pseudonymous player/entity identifier, level scores and an avatar identifier. The service also receives technical connection information, including an IP address. Ranked scores, the online display name returned by the service and avatar information can be visible to other players.

The game's installation identifier is generated for the app. It identifies the installation to the leaderboard service rather than requesting your real-world identity. Online functionality requires an internet connection. Your locally saved progress and scores are also stored on the device.

3. Advertising and consent

Ram Dhanush contains Google AdMob advertising, including banner ads, interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads. Rewards from rewarded ads are provided through the advertising service's reward callback.

Google's Mobile Ads SDK can collect and share information for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This includes:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location.
  • App and advertising interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views.
  • Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch time, hang rate and energy use.
  • Device or account identifiers, such as the advertising ID and app set ID, where available.

Advertising data processing depends on the SDK, your device settings, consent choices and applicable requirements. The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent where applicable. You can use Android's advertising settings to reset or delete the advertising ID. Google explains its data handling and available controls in the links below.

4. Why information is used

Information is used to provide game features, remember progress and preferences, show online rankings, display and measure advertising, provide ad-related rewards, diagnose technical problems and help prevent abuse or fraud. We do not ask for access to your contacts, precise GPS location, microphone or camera for gameplay.

5. Sharing you choose

If you use the game's sharing feature, a gameplay image can be saved temporarily on your device and passed to the app you choose through Android's share interface. Your chosen sharing app handles that content under its own terms and privacy policy.

6. Third-party services and hosting

Third-party providers process information under their own policies and service arrangements. Their processing may occur in countries other than your own.

7. Retention and security

Local game data is retained on the device until it is cleared, removed or otherwise changed. Online scores and associated identifiers may be retained by the leaderboard service until removed under the service's controls or a verified request. Advertising providers retain information according to their policies and applicable settings. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate security, fraud-prevention or legal reasons.

The game's configured leaderboard requests use HTTPS. Google states that Mobile Ads SDK user data is encrypted in transit using TLS. No system can guarantee absolute security.

8. Your choices and deletion requests

You can clear locally stored game data through Android's app-storage settings. This resets progress and local preferences; it does not automatically remove records already held by an online provider. Android advertising controls and Google's privacy tools offer additional advertising-data choices.

Request deletion of Ram Dhanush online data

Email playpixelinteractive1809@gmail.com with the subject Ram Dhanush data deletion request. State that you want your online game/leaderboard data deleted, and include any in-game player or support identifier you have. Do not send a password. We may need further information to identify the correct pseudonymous record and verify the request.

We will review requests to access, correct or remove data and explain any records that must be retained or provider-specific steps that apply.

9. Intended age group

Ram Dhanush is intended for players aged 13 and above and is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information through the game, contact us so that the situation can be reviewed. Applicable privacy and consent requirements can vary by country.

10. Support and changes to this policy

If you email support, the information you send, including your email address and message, is used to respond to your request. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information. We may update this policy as the game or its services change. The effective date above will identify the current version.

Contact: playpixelinteractive1809@gmail.com.
